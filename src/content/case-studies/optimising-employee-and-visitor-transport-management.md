### The Story

We partnered with Wilson James to solve a complex logistical challenge for one of their high-profile corporate clients. Poor public transport made commuting for over 3,000 employees difficult, and visitor transport from Edinburgh Airport was disorganised, relying on phone calls, emails, and manual schedules. The system caused inefficiencies and last-minute changes, hampering the smooth operation of shuttle services.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Optimising-Employee-and-Visitor-Transport-Management-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Fleetr streamlined transport operations, completing over 2,000 trips and tracking 50,000 miles within seven months. The app reduced fuel consumption by 10%, improved efficiency with 100% uptime, and reduced staffing needs for administration. The client now plans to expand Fleetr to cover more vehicles in the UK.

### Methodology

Fleetr was developed as a user-friendly, intuitive app available on both iOS and Android platforms. It enables dynamic task management for drivers, providing real-time updates on assignments and allowing for quick edits when needed. The app integrates live driver locations with Google Maps, giving management full visibility of fleet activity. Intelligent dispatching helps optimise vehicle operations, reducing time and resource wastage. Fleetr enhances the user experience by offering real-time updates on transport status, while instant messaging keeps drivers, employees, and management connected for smooth communication and efficient operations.
