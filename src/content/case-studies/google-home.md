### The Story

Google Home was looking for a partner to help them launch their new solution into the market. Chesamel successfully developed a high-impact campaign, achieving up to 3 million views per video and widespread engagement across social media.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Google-Home-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The campaign garnered up to 3 million views per video and achieved substantial engagement with domestic, tech-savvy audiences, including parents, seniors, and pet owners.

### Methodology

We created and distributed engaging, localised content filmed entirely with Nest Cameras across YouTube, The Dodo, and NowThis social media channels. The campaign featured endorsements from celebrity judges, prize-winning add-ons including a custom trophy by Jo Brimble, and unique hashtags (#NestPets & #Grruffs2021) to boost awareness.
