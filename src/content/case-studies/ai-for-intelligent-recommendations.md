### The Story

Despite having limited data availability, this client wanted to provide users with intelligent recommendations, while complying with the relevant data regulations and maintaining a great customer experience. Chesamel partnered with the business to build a GenAI module of their personal CRM, enabling them to give smart recommendations.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AI-for-Intelligent-Recommendations-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The solution delivered a personalised messaging strategy for users, with flexible and efficient execution, comprehensive user activity handling, and seamless CRM integration, making the technology easy to operate for the client.

### Methodology

The GenAI model offered a notification-centric approach using AWS Lambda, MongoDB, and the specialised LLM Anthropic’s Claude through Amazon Bedrock.
