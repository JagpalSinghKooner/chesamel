### The Story

Malaysia Blue Ocean Strategy Institute (MBOSI) is both an academic institute and a think tank that applies Blue Ocean Strategy to create impactful, cost-efficient solutions. To support its mission of helping the public sector achieve long-term goals, we partnered with MBOSI to build a global team of experts. We were tasked with sourcing international specialists to join MBOSI’s team in Kuala Lumpur, ensuring they could deliver cutting-edge strategies to their clients.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Building-a-Global-Team-for-Strategic-Impact-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Our recruitment efforts, tailored to the Asian market, allowed MBOSI to quickly assemble a high-caliber team of international specialists. By implementing the 'Zero Time to Hire' approach, the process was streamlined, ensuring rapid recruitment and deployment of talent. This enabled MBOSI to maintain its focus on delivering impactful and quickly executed solutions to public sector challenges.

### Methodology

Our method involved an agile and focused search process to identify and onboard international specialists who align with MBOSI's strategic goals. By leveraging local insights and global recruitment expertise, we ensured the rapid assembly of a skilled team, capable of delivering high-impact, low-cost solutions in line with Blue Ocean Strategy principles.
