### The Story

This client was struggling to make informed decisions using their existing data, so came to Chesamel asking for a solution that was tailor-made and easy to use. The business needed to be able to turn their data into actionable, clear insights.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Increasing-informed-decisions-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Using Microsoft Power BI's visualisation capabilities, the business unlocked the ability to identify trends and insights in their data that were previously hidden, leading to improved decision-making and therefore an uptick in business performanance.

### Methodology

We proposed and implemented a solution using Microsoft Power BI, which provided a centralised platform to track and analyse data in real-time.
