### The Story

This hyper-growth e-commerce company wanted to reduce the time their team spent on data analysis. Chesamel helped the client implement Looker to streamline their data reporting, resulting in significant revenue growth and time savings.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Data-Centralisation-with-Looker-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Looker's customised dashboards helped the business achieve a 25% increase in revenue and a 30% decrease in time spent on data analysis.

### Methodology

We helped the client implement Looker to create a centralised data hub with customised dashboards and reports, integrating data from all sources to provide real-time insights on sales, inventory, and customer behaviour.
