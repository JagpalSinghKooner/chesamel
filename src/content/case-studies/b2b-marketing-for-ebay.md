### The Story

Wanting to raise awareness amongst SMBs and large businesses, eBay came to us for support on a cross-border marketing campaign. We embedded a senior marketer onto the project, leading to increased engagement and revenue growth from target accounts.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/B2B-Marketing-for-eBay-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The campaign successfully achieved its objectives, driving revenue growth and increasing the number of SMBs and large merchants on the platform.

### Methodology

We embedded a senior marketer within eBay’s EU Seller Marketing team, managing the talent acquisition process and providing training and coaching to ensure successful execution of the 12-month campaign.
