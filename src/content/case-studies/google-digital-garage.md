### The Story

The Google Digital Garage is a nonprofit program designed to help people improve their digital skills with training, courses and certifications. Chesamel managed and delivered over 100,000 sessions for the Google Digital Garage, overseeing all aspects of installation and operations across multiple locations from 2015 to 2018.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Google-Digital-Garage-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Together, we successfully delivered over 100,000 individual and group sessions, solidifying Chesamel as the principal delivery partner for Google Digital Garage from 2015 to 2018.

### Methodology

Chesamel managed all aspects of the project including coordination, staffing, and day-to-day operations, across multiple locations and flagship retail sites. The service operated seven days a week for up to 12 months at each location.
