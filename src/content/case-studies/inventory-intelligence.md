### The Story

This client needed to improve the accuracy of its demand forecasting, using machine learning to handle its large volumes of data with a high number of SKUs. To address this challenge, Chesamel worked to come up with an innovative solution for Phantom Inventory and other related inventory anomalies.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Inventory-Intelligence-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The solution improved sales and returns recording, as well as inventory audit frequency, leading to a better customer experience and more insightful, actionable demand forecasting. As well as capturing $187M in incremental revenue, the client achieved >79% true positive predictions and <20% false positive predictions.

### Methodology

Our Artificial Inventory Intelligence product was developed to help retailers identify location or SKU combinations that are candidates for stock-outs.
