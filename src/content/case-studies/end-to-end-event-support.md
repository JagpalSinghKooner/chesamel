### The Story

When embarking on four major customer events, Google Cloud's EMEA and Web3 team were looking for support with day-to-day event management. Chesamel provided streamlined project management and marketing support for the team, streamlining operations and delivering the strategic customer events across Europe.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/End-to-end-Event-Support-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The project allowed Google Cloud to focus on strategy, streamline marketing operations, and gain additional marketing resources, providing greater flexibility for business needs.

### Methodology

Chesamel managed end-to-end logistics, third-party vendors, and event production, ensuring all visual collateral aligned with Google brand guidelines. We assigned a dedicated Marketing Account Manager with B2B multichannel experience to handle operations, provide flexible assistance with ad-hoc requests, and leverage close relationships with existing vendors to maximise efficiency.
