### The Story

This business needed to speed up its operational decisions by having a clearer picture of real-time performance, so set out to find a visualisation solution that could easily slot into its existing business processes.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Visualising-data-in-real-time-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

With automated data consolidation and reporting processes, the business was provided with actionable insights to optimise business performance, saving their team significant time and resources to focus on strategic initiatives.

### Methodology

Chesamel set up a centralised data hub for the organisation, integrating data from all sources and creating customised dashboards and reports that could give real-time insights into the client's day-to-day business.
