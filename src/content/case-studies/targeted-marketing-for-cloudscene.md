### The Story

We helped Cloudscene refine its business proposition through social media, direct calls, email marketing, and other marketing strategies, helping the business secure contracts with major brands through a targeted marketing strategy.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Targeted-Marketing-for-Cloudscene-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The campaign led to business agreements with major brands like Peugeot, Metro Bank, and BDO, with each contract valued at approximately £100K per annum. These partnerships significantly boosted Cloudscene’s market presence and validated their product offering.

### Methodology

Chesamel implemented a comprehensive marketing strategy including direct outreach, email marketing, and social media campaigns to secure director-level meetings with prominent brands. Additionally, we offered product trials to prospective clients to demonstrate the value of Cloudscene's digital signage product.
