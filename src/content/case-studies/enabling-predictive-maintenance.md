### The Story

This specialist ShipTech data analytics client wanted to improve its predictive maintenance capabilities in order to speed up operations and innovate its technology posture. Chesamel helped the business and its team members carry out this data transformation.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Enabling-predictive-maintenance-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Our carefully designed AI solution increased data clarity, which helped to optimise vessel performance significantly, reducing fuel consumption and decreasing CO2 emissions. The client also initiated patenting of the new solution.

### Methodology

Chesamel crafted an AI solution for this data transformation, combining a fusion of both ML-based and physics-based models. We utilised our AI expertise to replace outdated rule-based and statistical models.
