### The Story

This Japanese asset management firm wanted to improve its key metrics and launch a new investment product. Chesamel worked with the business to boost these metrics using an AI-powered research environment.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AI-powered-Asset-Management-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Metrics were improved across drawdowns with increased annual returns from 6% to 13%, and enhanced risk management capabilities. The business achieved a 25% magnitude of drawdowns, down from 32%, and a 0.9 shape ratio, up from 0.4.

### Methodology

Chesamel implemented a cloud-based research environment using macroeconomic and fundamental stock data, with an AI-powered hierarchical portfolio clustering technique to improve risk representation.
