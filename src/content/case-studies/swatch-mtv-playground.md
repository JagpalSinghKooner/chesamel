### The Story

The Swatch MTV Playground is a platform for emerging artists to showcase their work across different mediums. Chesamel partnered with Swatch and MTV to launch the campaign by implementing a strategic media relations and influencer campaign that enhanced brand reputation and set a new standard for digital art engagement.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Swatch-MTV-Playground-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The campaign revolutionised digital art engagement, significantly driving awareness, enhancing brand reputation, and influencing the continued evolution of digital platforms, including YouTube and TikTok.

### Methodology

Chesamel collaborated with MTV to develop and execute strategic campaigns in key locations, incorporating influencer campaigns, talent management, and a PR strategy across two continents. Using our embedded workforce solutions, we provided the necessary skills and remote management to ensure the successful execution of the strategy with minimal client effort.
