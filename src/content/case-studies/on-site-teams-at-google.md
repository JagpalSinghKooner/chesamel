### The Story

Google needed a flexible recruitment solution that would provide them with the right professionals for both short term and long term projects. We provided Google with on-site experts, enabling efficient project execution and scalability without affecting headcount.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/On-site-teams-at-Google-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The solution enabled Google to scale quickly without impacting headcount, enhancing collaboration within project environments and establishing a reliable partnership for marketing expertise.

### Methodology

Chesamel proposed and implemented a model where highly skilled on-site experts, managed and trained by us, seamlessly integrated into Google teams. This approach allowed for specialised project execution and strategic alignment with Google's objectives.
