### The Story

Chesamel partnered with YouTube and Vogue to launch Vogue Visionaries, a masterclass series designed to inspire and educate through insights from the UK’s top creative minds.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Vogue-Visionaries-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The successful launch of Vogue Visionaries provided a unique educational platform, enhancing both YouTube's and Vogue’s influence in the creative industry.

### Methodology

Chesamel managed the project from end to end, handling stakeholder management, reviewing and providing creative feedback, writing briefs, and overseeing owend and operating outputs to ensure a seamless and impactful launch.
