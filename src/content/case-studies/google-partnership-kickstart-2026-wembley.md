Event Production & Experience

### The Story

Partner Kickstart 2026 required a seamless, high-scale experience for Google Cloud teams and partners, delivered inside a complex venue environment with strict global brand standards. The challenge was to move people smoothly, keep momentum high, and ensure every physical and digital touchpoint (screens, signage, badges, lanyards, backdrops, printed assets) felt consistent, premium, and intentional.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Partner-Kickstart-5301-1024x683.jpg)

## Impact

We enabled a fast, frictionless experience from first check-in to final pack-down, including:

- **400+ attendees registered in 30 minutes**, maintaining flow despite venue constraints
- **100% brand-compliant collateral** across apparel, lanyards, badges, bespoke signage, and partner assets
- **Operational efficiency end-to-end**, from morning registration through **8:00 PM pack-down**, clearing the venue on schedule

![](/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Partner-Kickstart-2329-1024x683.jpg)

### Methodology

  
**Turnkey infrastructure & on-site delivery**

- Managed equipment logistics, technical build, staging, and on-site production oversight to standard  
    

**Wayfinding & experience flow**

- Implemented a structured environmental system (including colour-coded assets and bespoke signage) to optimise movement and support targeted partner networking  
    

**Front-of-house registration operations**

- Delivered registration management, staffing, and technical oversight to keep throughput high and guest experience consistent  
    

**Post-event intelligence**

- Provided a structured debrief identifying technical and production efficiencies to reduce friction for future large-scale installs
