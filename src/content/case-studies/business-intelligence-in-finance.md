### The Story

A financial services business wanted to improve business decision making and increase ROI by better analysing and managing data. This data was stored in disparate systems, including legacy databases and cloud-based platforms. The organisation therefore needed a BI model that could understand and manage this data in real time, boosting business growth.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Business-Intelligence-in-Finance-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The implementation of BigQuery revealed hidden trends, patterns, and anomalies in the client's data in real time, leading to improved business decision-making and a reported 43% ROI.

### Methodology

The Chesamel team implemented Google BigQuery to provide real-time data visualisation and analysis capabilities. We set up a centralised data hub and created customised dashboards and reports that provided real-time insights.
