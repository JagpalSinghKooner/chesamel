### The Story

Google Cloud wanted to inform C-suite decision-makers on its smart data usage and how it can help to enhance resilience in real-world business scenarios. Chesamel collaborated with Google Cloud and the Financial Times to create an immersive, narrative-driven web experience that demonstrated how it helps drive sustainability-focused business transformation for C-suite decision-makers.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Bringing-Sustainability-to-Life-for-Google-Cloud-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

The campaign successfully brought Google Cloud’s sustainability narrative to life, offering an engaging, educational experience for decision-makers across EMEA. It positioned Google Cloud as a leader in driving business resilience and sustainability through data intelligence. The campaign garnered 45,514 page views, 3,912 social engagements, and 10,234 game plays.

### Methodology

Chesamel developed a web-based interactive experience where users made key decisions for the fictional Sunrise Cereals Co., using narrative-first design and smart gameplay mechanics to illustrate the business impact of data and AI. The experience included 50 "smart points" for decision-making and was designed to be engaging, visually appealing, and accessible across devices.
