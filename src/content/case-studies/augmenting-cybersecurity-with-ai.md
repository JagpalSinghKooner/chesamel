### The Story

This innovative cybersecurity management services clients needed to address the challenge of complying with multiple, disparate regulations. Chesamel helped the client automate manual compliance with an AI-powered platform.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Augmenting-Cybersecurity-with-AI-1-1024x576.png)

### Impact

Thanks to automated infrastructure provisioning, new tenants can now be deployed in under 30 minutes, radically reducing time required to produce deliverables like audit reports.

### Methodology

The AI platform was created through Amazon BedRock using LLMs with two main features: Audit AI SAAS platform and RFP response preparation SaaS platform with GenAI.
