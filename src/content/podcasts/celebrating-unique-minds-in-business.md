Diversity is key to success, from diversity of thinking, creating and doing a different approach is what powers innovation. Studies reveal that companies with inclusive cultures are 6 times more likely to drive innovation and agility. In our most recent episode, we explore the power of neurodiversity in business. Our guest speaker, Sonay Erten, a self-employed consultant solicitor advocate at Legal Studio Solicitors, shares practical strategies for fostering an inclusive work environment that values and supports neurodivergent individuals, through her personal journey.
