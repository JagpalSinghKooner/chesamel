Our CEO John Cofie sits down with Eugene Pataliak, Managing Partner at Wezom, to explore the evolving world of business transformation. John and Eugene dive into real-world examples—like how AI is streamlining logistics and even saving lives—and discuss the challenges businesses face when embracing digital transformation. They also unpack what it really takes for companies to modernise, from cloud readiness to building the right teams, and share personal reflections on growth, mistakes, and the future of tech. A conversation about the people who drive change.
