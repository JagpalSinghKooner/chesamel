Chesamel, a London-based consultancy with deep expertise in go-to-market strategy, project delivery, and workforce enablement, has announced a strategic partnership with [Systango](https://www.systango.com/), a digital engineering firm specialising in scalable, AI-powered software solutions. The collaboration unites the unique strengths of both organisations: technical precision with consulting excellence. 

“Most transformation efforts focus on the output - new tools, new tech, new processes,” said John Cofie, CEO of Chesamel. “But successful transformation is rooted in clarity and people. We bring the insight to identify the real challenge, the experience to shape the right response, and the capability to deliver it end-to-end. Our partnership with Systango gives clients everything they need in one agile, integrated solution.”

Systango will lead the development of secure, enterprise-grade platforms, while Chesamel ensures those systems are adopted, embedded, and aligned with business goals. Their combined approach is designed to bridge the gap between innovation and execution, pairing engineering excellence with the strategic clarity, enablement, and modular transformation capabilities required to create lasting impact at scale.

“One of the biggest blockers to digital transformation isn’t the technology, it’s adoption,” said **Vinita Rathi**, CEO of Systango. “By combining our engineering expertise with Chesamel’s strength in go-to-market execution, stakeholder enablement, and cultural integration, we can shorten the gap between innovation and adoption.”

Together, Chesamel and Systango will offer organisations a more integrated, insight-led approach to digital transformation.

### About Chesamel Group

Chesamel is a transformation consultancy that blends marketing, change management, and talent activation to help brands accelerate growth. With deep expertise in digital adoption, employee enablement, and go-to-market strategy, Chesamel supports organisations in making new technologies stick - internally, in-market, and at scale. 

### About Systango

Systango is a London-headquartered, publicly traded digital engineering consultancy that has helped over 450 companies rapidly build and scale secure, enterprise-grade software solutions. One of the first 20 companies in the world to receive the GEN AI specialisation by Google, Systango is known for delivering high-performance, AI-powered platforms that prioritise security, usability, and business value.
