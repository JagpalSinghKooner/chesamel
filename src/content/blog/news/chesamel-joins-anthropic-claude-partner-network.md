We’re pleased to announce that Chesamel has officially joined the Anthropic Claude Partner Network.

It’s an important milestone for our team, but it’s also a natural next step. Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, is the model provider powering agentic solutions we’ve already built for clients. Joining the network formalises a relationship that is already active in our delivery work.

### **From production experience to formal partnership**

Many partnership announcements begin with what might happen next. Ours starts with work that’s already underway.

We’ve been using Claude to help build agentic solutions designed around real business workflows. This isn’t technology sitting in a demonstration environment. It’s being applied to practical client challenges, with scalability, governance and long-term value in mind.

Joining the Anthropic Claude Partner Network gives that experience a stronger formal foundation. It creates new opportunities for our team to deepen its expertise and bring the latest implementation knowledge into our client work.

### **What the Anthropic Claude Partner Network adds**

Through the network, Chesamel gains access to Claude certification opportunities, implementation best practices and Anthropic’s wider partner ecosystem.

In practical terms, this will help us:

- Deepen our team’s Claude expertise through structured certification pathways.
- Apply Anthropic’s implementation guidance to our delivery approach.
- Learn from and contribute to a wider ecosystem of AI specialists and partners.
- Bring new knowledge and capabilities directly into live client programmes.

It strengthens our ability to deliver AI transformation that is scalable, responsible and built to last.

### **What this means for our clients**

Most businesses don’t need more AI theory. They need to understand where AI can create meaningful value, how to put it into production and how to scale it without losing control.

Becoming part of the Claude Partner Network supports us in doing exactly that. Our practical delivery experience is now backed by closer access to Anthropic’s expertise, resources and partner community.

For our clients, that means working with a team that can connect Claude’s capabilities to their strategy, workflows and intended outcomes. Whether the opportunity involves testing an initial agentic use case or expanding AI across multiple functions, we can help create a practical route from ambition to implementation.

### **Strengthening our digital proposition**

This partnership directly supports Triple Stack, Chesamel’s digital and AI proposition designed to move enterprise AI into production through clear use cases, governed architecture and accountable delivery.

Claude brings advanced reasoning capabilities into agentic solutions. Chesamel connects those capabilities to the wider delivery picture, including strategy, data, governance, people and implementation.

The goal isn’t simply to introduce another AI tool. It’s to build solutions that work within the realities of an organisation and create measurable value.

### **What comes next**

Joining the Anthropic Claude Partner Network is a meaningful step, but it isn’t the finish line.

We’ll continue investing in the people, expertise and technology partnerships that help our clients put AI to work responsibly and effectively. We’re excited to build on what’s already in production and explore what becomes possible next.

If you’re considering how Claude or agentic AI could improve a priority workflow, speak to our team about a practical route from idea to implementation.
