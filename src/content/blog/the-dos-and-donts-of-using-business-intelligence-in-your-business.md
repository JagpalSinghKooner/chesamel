Business Intelligence (BI) is now a crucial component of almost every successful business operation. With more than 83% of companies now employing a Chief Data Officer (CDO) or Chief Data and Analytics Officer (compared to 12% in 2012), the reliance on BI to inform decision-making is at an all-time high.

But while BI offers enormous potential, it also comes with challenges that can hinder its effectiveness if not handled correctly. To fully harness the power of BI, businesses must be strategic in their approach, avoiding common mistakes and adhering to best practices.

After launching our BI & Data Analytics offering, the team at Chesamel have helped hundreds of different sized companies maximise their business intelligence. Over that time, they have gathered a concrete list of the do’s and don’t of using BI successfully.

Let’s take a look at them in detail.

## The Do’s of Using Business Intelligence

### 1\. Do Define Clear Objectives

Before diving into BI, it’s essential to establish what you want to achieve. Clear objectives and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) are the foundation of a successful BI strategy. 

Whether your goal is to improve customer satisfaction, increase operational efficiency, or boost sales, setting these objectives upfront ensures that your BI efforts are focused and aligned with your business goals.

### 2\. Do Invest in the Right Tools For Your Business

Selecting the right BI tools is crucial for gathering actionable insights. Whether you opt for traditional BI systems, self-service BI, cloud-based solutions, or mobile BI, the tools should align with your business needs and be scalable to accommodate growth. Cloud-based BI solutions, for example, offer flexibility and cost-efficiency, making them an excellent choice for businesses of all sizes.

### 3\. Do Foster a Data-Driven Culture

For BI to be effective, it must be integrated into the company culture. This means encouraging collaboration across departments and ensuring that all employees understand the value of data in decision-making. Companies like Amazon have successfully embedded data-driven decision-making into their operations, leading to innovative solutions and a competitive edge.

## The Don’ts of Using Business Intelligence

### 1\. Don’t Rely on Outdated Data

The accuracy and timeliness of your data are paramount. Using outdated or incorrect data can lead to flawed decisions that negatively impact your business. It’s essential to ensure that your BI systems are fed with up-to-date, clean data to provide accurate and actionable insights.

### 2\. Don’t Overcomplicate the Process

BI tools can generate vast amounts of data, but it’s essential to focus on the metrics that matter most to your business. Overcomplicating your BI strategy by trying to analyse everything can lead to information overload, making it harder to draw meaningful conclusions. Instead, prioritise key metrics that align with your objectives.

### 3\. Don’t Neglect Data Security

As your business increasingly relies on data, protecting that data becomes even more critical. Data breaches can have severe consequences, both financially and to your reputation. Implementing strong data security measures, such as encryption and regular audits, is vital to safeguarding your BI data and maintaining trust with your customers.

## The Impact of Effective BI on Business Success

When implemented correctly, BI can transform your business by improving decision-making, enhancing operational efficiency, and providing a competitive advantage. Companies with robust BI systems are not only more likely to make faster decisions but also more likely to outperform their competitors in financial performance.

In fact, businesses that leverage data-driven decision-making are 5% more productive and 6% more profitable than their peers. These figures underscore the importance of getting BI right.
