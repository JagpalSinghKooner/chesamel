This might attract some heat in the comments, but is it time to admit that the analogue world is gone for good? 

Some might say yes. After attending Google Cloud Summit London last week, we’re inclined to agree. 

Surrounded by over 3000 fellow Google customers and partners on a gloriously sunny London day, the temperature wasn’t the only thing that was borderline overwhelming. 

Listening to over 400 customer success stories and witnessing the latest Google Cloud innovations made one thing abundantly clear: we are on the cusp of something *huge*. 

AI and ML are set to reshape how we work, create, and interact at a scale we haven’t experienced before, and Chesamel is already thinking about the infinite possibilities they present.  

Showcasing 11 interactive demos spanning multi-modal AI and ML applications from coding to video production to Formula E race-car driving, for attendees, feeling the ‘magic behind the magic’ made the possibilities feel tangible…and surprisingly close.

Traversing the Google Cloud AI playground and seeing how digital transformation enabled participants to create short films from a single sketch and build a functional app with just one short phone call would make it impossible not to start thinking about all the diverse real-world applications these digital quantum leaps will bring to life. 

That is…if the right strategy is in place. Demos alone don’t transform businesses. Strategy does.

Exploring these new capabilities only reinforces the undeniable truth that sustainable AI adoption and wide-scale digital transformation can only move as fast as the minds behind it. Success is as reliant on getting the right people behind the right products and driving the right processes as it is on technology. 

Witnessing how quickly teams could create apps, manipulate media, and engage robotics was thrilling. But without the right framework, insight, and adoption models, innovation remains a showroom fantasy.

That’s where the real transformation begins, and where Chesamel operates, bridging the gap between vision and reality.
