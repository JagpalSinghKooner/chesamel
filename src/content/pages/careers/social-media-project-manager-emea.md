## Role Responsibilities:

EMEA Social strategy development — 30%

- Work with the Head of Social Media EMEA and the Social Media Lead to shape and evolve the EMEA social strategy, aligned with global priorities and our clients Cloud objectives.
- Translate strategic priorities into clear recommendations for regional activity, campaign focus and channel use.
- Identify gaps, opportunities and competing priorities, bringing recommendations to the Head of Social Media EMEA for decisions.

Regional planning and guidance — 20%

- Support regional social media managers in developing realistic quarterly and monthly content plans.
- Help local social media teams prioritise requests and balance campaign, local and always-on content.
- Coordinate cross-regional requests, including but not limited to markets such as Nordics and Israel, with clear owners, briefs and deadlines.
- Identify opportunities to introduce clients to other Chesamel capabilities across talent, marketing, technology, AI and workforce transformation.
- Flag content volumes or requests that exceed available capacity and propose adjustments.

Content quality and direction — 15%

- Define what good social content looks like across themes, formats, cadence and storytelling.
- Review priority content and provide specific, actionable feedback on messaging, audience relevance and quality.
- Apply editorial judgement to all content and recommendations, including AI-assisted work, before sharing them.
- Support regional managers to improve their own output while retaining ownership of execution.

Performance review and optimisation — 15%

- Review regional performance and identify trends, gaps and opportunities.
- Recommend practical changes to content, formats and planning based on results.
- Work with regional managers to test improvements and share useful learning across markets.

Stakeholder collaboration and workflow oversight — 10%

- Act as a strategic partner to the Head of Social Media EMEA and collaborate with the Social Media Lead, regional managers and relevant stakeholders.
- Maintain visibility of assigned work in Airtable, including status, ownership, approvals and blockers.
- Coordinate reviews and follow up on sign-offs for agreed workstreams.
- Take ownership of selected tactical deliverables where agreed, with clear priorities and capacity.

**Best practice and framework development — 10%**

- Develop practical planning templates, content guidance and review frameworks.
- Turn recurring issues into clearer processes or guidance for the team.
- Keep guidance aligned with our clients current requirements, seeking clarification from the appropriate owner where needed.

Proposed division of responsibilities

- **Stakeholder** sets strategic priorities, confirms scope and resolves competing demands.
- **Senior Strategy Lead L**eads day-to-day team coordination, ongoing mentorship and operational unblocking, with the division of content review and approvals to be confirmed.
- **Strategist:** owns strategic development, planning guidance, quality standards, performance recommendations and agreed workflow oversight.
- **Regional Managers:** own local planning, content development and execution within the agreed framework.

## Top 3 must have skills

**Strategic thinking and commercial understanding** — translating Cloud’s priorities into practical regional social plans, then using performance insights to improve them.

**Editorial judgement and content direction** — recognising what makes strong B2B social content and giving specific, useful feedback that improves messaging, storytelling and audience relevance.

**Ownership, prioritisation and collaboration** — managing competing requests, being transparent about capacity and proactively resolving blockers, while helping regional managers work independently.

Nice to have skills/Qualifications:

- **Technology or cloud-sector experience:** familiarity with B2B technology audiences and translating complex topics into engaging social content.
- **Multi-market experience:** supporting regional teams across EMEA and adapting global strategy to local audiences.
- **Social tools and analytics:** experience with Airtable, Sprinklr or similar workflow, publishing and reporting platforms.
- **Mentoring experience:** coaching social media managers and developing practical content guidance or frameworks.
- **Responsible use of AI:** using AI to support research and content development, with strong editorial judgement and quality checks.
- **Relevant qualification:** a degree or professional qualification in marketing, communications or digital strategy, or equivalent practical experience.
- **Additional European languages:** useful for regional collaboration, particularly Spanish, German or French.

Client culture considerations/ways of working

- Maintain a transparent view of projects, priorities and time commitments.
- Raise capacity concerns early, identifying the affected deliverables and proposing trade-offs.
- Agree changes in ownership or delegation with the relevant lead.
- Give regional managers clear guidance and appropriate autonomy.
- Keep routine requests within working hours and use out-of-hours escalation for genuinely urgent matters.
